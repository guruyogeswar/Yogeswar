Primary Project

Yggdrasil — Cognitive Architecture for Autonomous Agents

Founder & Sole Engineer · Jun 2025 – Present

Yggdrasil is a biologically-grounded cognitive architecture built to address a core limitation in modern game AI: scripted NPC logic that cannot scale with world complexity. It explores whether persistent internal state, memory decay, and social consequence can produce unscripted long-term agent behavior—without explicit reward functions or behavior trees.

  • Built ~30,000 lines of production C++ in 4 months (simulation engine, cognition, telemetry).
  • Engineered agents driven by energy constraints, internal drives, and memory—removing the need for hand-authored behavior trees.
  • Executed 100,000+ tick simulations with multi-generation population stability.
  • Observed unscripted emergence under scarcity: specialization, trade, signaling, and territory formation.
  • Identified a failure mode (“behavioral collapse”) where cognition harms survival in overly stable environments.
  • Filed a provisional patent to establish priority on an early version of the architecture.
