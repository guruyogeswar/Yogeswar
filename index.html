Primary Project

Yggdrasil: Cognitive Architecture for Autonomous Agents

Founder & Sole Engineer | Jun 2025 – Present

Yggdrasil is a biologically inspired cognitive architecture designed to overcome the limitations of traditional game AI. Instead of relying on scripted logic, it uses persistent internal state, memory decay, and social consequences to enable agents to act and adapt naturally over time—without explicit reward functions or behavior trees.

  • Developed a 30,000-line C++ codebase in 4 months, including simulation engine, cognition, and telemetry.
  • Created agents driven by energy, internal drives, and memory, eliminating the need for hand-authored behavior trees.
  • Ran large-scale simulations (100,000+ ticks) with stable multi-generational populations.
  • Observed emergent behaviors such as specialization, trade, signaling, and territory formation under resource scarcity.
  • Discovered a "behavioral collapse" failure mode where advanced cognition can reduce survival in overly stable environments.
  • Filed a provisional patent for the architecture.
